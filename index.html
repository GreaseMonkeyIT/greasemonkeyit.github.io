About

      

      [ BIO.LOG ]
    


    

      

        

          I am a pre-final year B.Tech student in Electrical and Electronics Engineering at NIT Andhra Pradesh, with a minor in AI for Signal Processing. My work includes writing bare-metal Linux drivers, designing PCBs, developing RTL processors, and training neural networks on industrial sensor data.
        

        

          I am a founding member at ASTA Health Tech, a medtech startup. As Founding Embedded Engineer; I have co-developed the full hardware and BSP stack at our organization. I have interned at Cummins Turbo Technologies under the Electrical Maintenance Department, and am currently interning at Tata Hitachi Construction Machinery under the Design & Development Department.
        

        

          At NIT-ANP; I am the Joint-Secretary of the AI & Robotics Club, and have run and organized multiple events, including our college's inaugural Hackathon - Hackademia. I have also competed with my team in various events, and placed as a finalist at SIH 2024 (ISRO-SAC), and AIR 7 at FMAE FKDC.
        

        

          Outside engineering I have a long-standing interest in retro computing, video games, and competitive hardware challenges.
