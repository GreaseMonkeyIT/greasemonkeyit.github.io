AIR 7
Autocross — APPS-BSE plausibility fault
The APPS-BSE (Accelerator Pedal Position Sensor / Brake System Encoder) interlock is a mandatory EV safety requirement: if brake and throttle are simultaneously active beyond threshold, the motor must cut within 500ms. During the autocross run, the BSE falsely reported brake engagement under high-vibration conditions, triggering a spurious motor cut mid-course. The driver manually disconnected the interlock wire and completed the course, but the run time was outside the 107% delta, classifying the event as a non-score run. All other dynamic events ran clean. The kart's handling performance in skidpad and lateral acceleration demonstrates the underlying vehicle dynamics were not the issue.
Vehicle Specifications

Full build overview

Chassis
Perimeter Frame
AISI 1018 seamless mild steel, 1" OD × 2.5mm wall, arc-welded in-house. 44" wheelbase, 64×60×44" overall. FEA in ANSYS: min safety factor 1.15 at front mount, all deformations elastic. Chosen over chromoly (weld difficulty).
Powertrain
QS138 90H BLDC + Chain Drive
4kW rated, 93% peak efficiency, 60+ Nm torque, IP67. 428 roller chain, 14T-38T sprockets (Gr = 2.71). Calculated top speed 101.3 km/h. 0-60 km/h in ~3s. Gear ratio selected after MATLAB trade-off: acceleration vs. top speed balance for FKDC dynamic events.
Battery Pack
72V 80Ah Dual-Module
Two 20s×10p modules (Molicel 21700, 4Ah, 45A continuous) wired in parallel: effective 20s×20p. 5.76 kWh usable. Peak discharge 32.4 kW. Symmetrically mounted for lateral balance. Passive air cooling, integrated BMS per module, UN38.3 certified.
Steering
Ackermann Geometry
95.13% Ackermann compliance. Inner lock 42.81°, outer lock 26.83°, average turning radius 2.06m. Twin tie-rod, no rack-and-pinion. Purely mechanical for simplicity and reliability. Steering ratio 2.021:1. 10° camber.
Brakes
Rear Hydraulic Disc
220mm cast iron disc, floating caliper, DOT 3 fluid, steel-braided lines. 5:1 pedal ratio, 400N driver input → 7.05 MPa hydraulic pressure. Stopping distance: 11.05m (dry), 25.79m (wet) from 100 km/h. Aluminium pedal rated to 2000N.
Safety
FKDC §20.2 / §20.3 Compliant
Dual kill switches (seat + column), BOTS, APPS-BSE interlock, metallic firewall, dual fire extinguishers, accumulator isolation with double relays. Power-off indicator light + 2s audible ready-to-drive signal. Full compliance earned 100/100 bonus points.

Electrical System

Power distribution & system integration

The electrical system integrates the battery pack, motor controller, safety interlocks, and instrumentation into a cohesive, regulation-compliant EV platform.

Motor Controller
ND72680
Matched to QS138 90H. Handles field-oriented control for the BLDC motor at 72V nominal. Hall sensor throttle input, LCD speedometer output, DC-DC converter for 12V aux systems. Configured for FKDC peak-torque and thermal limits.
Pack Architecture
Dual-Module 20s×20p
Two independent 20s×10p modules allow physical separation for lateral weight balance. Each module has its own BMS. Parallel connection at the main contactor. Peak pack current: 450A discharge, 135A charge. Internal resistance: 27.6 mΩ.
Protection Chain
BMS + Fusing + Interlock
Per-cell fuse: 56.3A. BMS thresholds: over-voltage 4.25V/cell, under-voltage 2.50V/cell, temp cut-off 60°C. Main contactor + accumulator isolation relays. Watt-meter analyser for live power monitoring. SOC tracked via coulomb counting.
Safety Interlocks
APPS-BSE / BOTS / Kill
APPS-BSE: motor cut within 500ms if brake + throttle simultaneously active. BOTS: motor cut on brake over-travel (hydraulic failure indicator). Dual kill switches: immediate traction cutoff, brake light remains live. All wiring 70mm² cable, steel-braided brake lines.
Key Design Decisions
AISI 1018 over 4130 chromoly
4130 offers higher yield strength but requires specialised welding, local unavailability drove up cost, and the strength advantage disappears when section geometry is equivalent. 1018 seamless pipe is weldable with standard MIG in-house, available locally, and the FEA confirmed adequate safety factors throughout - no reinforcements required post-analysis.
Symmetric dual battery module placement
A single large module is simpler but creates lateral CG offset. Splitting into two identical 20s×10p modules and mounting symmetrically on either side of the chassis eliminates side-bias during high-speed cornering. Each module also has its own BMS, providing redundancy - one module can be isolated without losing full drive capability.
14T-38T sprocket ratio (Gr = 2.71)
Two ratios were analysed: 2.62 and 3.15. Lower ratio (2.62) gives higher top speed; higher ratio (3.15) gives better low-end torque. FKDC events reward acceleration and cornering over outright top speed, so 2.71 (the 14T-38T combination) was chosen as the crossover point - sufficient torque for dynamic events while staying within the motor's RPM band at top speed.
